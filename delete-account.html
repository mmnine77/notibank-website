Delete your NotiBank account & data

NotiBank gives you control over your notification history, encrypted Google Drive backups, and account-linked information. You can request account deletion in the app or contact the developer if you no longer have access to the app.

Option 1 — Delete from the NotiBank app

  1. Open NotiBank and sign in with the Google account associated with your NotiBank data.
  2. Open Settings (Vault Settings).
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone and select Delete NotiBank Account.
  4. Read the deletion details, verify with your Recovery Password when requested, and confirm the deletion.

Deleting the NotiBank account is different from merely disconnecting your Google account or uninstalling the app.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access NotiBank, email the developer to request deletion of your NotiBank account and associated data. You can use the email button below after the developer configures the support address.

What data is deleted?

When the in-app account deletion completes successfully, the app is designed to remove the following NotiBank-associated data, as applicable:

  • NotiBank cloud notification backup archives associated with the Google account.
  • NotiBank encryption-key metadata and account recovery information managed by the app.
  • Account-linked device and synchronization records managed by NotiBank.
  • NotiBank account-linked local notification data on the device where deletion is performed.

Data stored locally on other devices may require separate removal on those devices. Deletion of a NotiBank account does not delete your Google account or unrelated files in Google Drive.

Data retention and separate deletion options

NotiBank also offers options to delete selected local records or cloud backups without deleting the entire account. Certain records placed in Cloud Trash may be retained for up to 30 days before permanent removal, depending on the deletion action chosen.

Full account deletion is intended to remove account-associated NotiBank cloud data and keys rather than simply moving them into ordinary temporary trash. Any legally required retention, if applicable, will be explained when processing an external deletion request.

Before publishing, the developer should verify these descriptions against the released app, especially cross-device local data and whether full account deletion bypasses Cloud Trash.
