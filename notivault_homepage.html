THE NOTIVAULT EXPERIENCE

Everything important, within reach.

A private place to save notifications, manage encrypted backups, and recover your history across devices you control.

Notification History

Keep past Android notifications available for review after they disappear from the notification shade.

Encrypted Google Drive Backup

Store encrypted backups in the Google Drive app data area linked to your account.

Multiple Devices

Access and restore your account's authorized backups on other Android devices using the recovery process.

Cloud Trash

Recover eligible deleted notification records from encrypted cloud trash during the retention period.

Smart Filters

Choose which apps to include and reduce unnecessary system notification noise.

Recovery Protection

Account recovery-password protection and on-device key management help safeguard encrypted archives.

Built around your control.

NotiVault requires permission to access notifications. Cloud backups are intended for your own Google account, with encrypted archives and recovery-password safeguards. Review the privacy policy to understand how information is handled.
